Rainy Day Arcade is the games shelf of Alex Price (StellarRequiem). Browser cabinets and small iOS apps, made for people who remember carpet, mixtapes, and Saturdays that stayed indoors.

Shipping status is printed on each cabinet. Coming soon means it is not in the store. The pictures in this kit are stand-ins, drawn for the page, until final icons and screenshots exist.
