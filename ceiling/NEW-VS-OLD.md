# New versus pre-existing work

The xclusivexo.com portfolio existed before the WebMCP Challenge submission
period. It is context, not the judged implementation.

## Pre-existing

- Portfolio navigation, visual language, papers, capability statement, and
  other public project pages.
- The broader Assurance Mesh research and its internal workflow artifacts.

## New during the challenge

- Shared page-level WebMCP public-information allowlist.
- The Ceiling user-agent workbench and interactive four-stage authority
  circuit, with deterministic policy evaluation inside proposal admission.
- The shared scalar change engine, release-policy adapter, generated
  schema contract, three unrelated synthetic adapters, and 50-case universal
  conformance matrix.
- The typed synthetic release-policy workflow, semantic before/after diff,
  deterministic risk assessment, and hard policy denials.
- Exact, bounded schemas and WebMCP security annotations.
- Payload-bound idempotency and stale-baseline refusal.
- Content-bound, one-use, expiring trusted page activation.
- Serialized deterministic receipt core and complete-chain verification.
- User-triggered export of a verified session evidence bundle.
- Agent-callable Defense Matrix with seven isolated deterministic probes
  against the implemented authority and receipt core.
- Regression fixtures for concurrency, mutation, deletion, reorder, expiry,
  wrong operation, wrong id, input validation, and registration failures.
- The 23-case release-policy evaluation, 50-case universal-adapter matrix, and
  30-prompt live-agent protocol.
- Public claim ledger, evidence notes, demo plan, and submission packet.

The public repository must preserve dated history from the submission period.
No commit or artifact should be backdated, and unrelated dirty portfolio work
must not be mixed into the challenge release.
