THE SHIP IS YOURS

Two hundred and forty days if you hold nominal. The levers below the gauges are the whole interface.
COAST / NOMINAL / BURN
How hard the drive works. Coast saves fuel and heat and makes the trip longer. Burn shortens it. Fuel and recycler heat rise with the square of throttle. Arriving at Mars still costs 26 fuel unless you fly the corridor.
SHORT / FULL
Meals. Short rations stretch calories and nothing else. Oxygen is not on that lever. Hungry crew lose health.
REPAIR
One part, into the worst module. An engineer makes it worth about 7.5%. Anyone else, about 4%. Keep the recycler up. When it falls, every day costs more water and oxygen than the last.
SALVAGE
A named crewmember goes outside. The suit breathes ship oxygen. Cyan is your velocity, amber is your thrust, Space is the brake. Esc does not bring them in. The airlock does.
EVENTS
The card tells you the numbers before you choose. The clock is stopped until you do.
