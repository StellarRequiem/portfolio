A NOTE ON RISK, FROM LEGAL.
A name on this manifest is a crew name like any other. It can be drawn as your pilot and it can be drawn as the one who does not make it — hypoxia, dehydration, a decompression event on day 171. Astraeus does not intervene in the draw and neither do we. A credit that cannot lose is not a credit, it is a logo. This one can lose.
BOARDING NOT YET OPEN

Berth sales are not live. When they open, this is where they will open from. Until then the register stands empty and honest — no placeholder colonists, no names that were never contributed.

THE REGISTER

ABOUT THE REGISTER.
Names are listed only with the agreement of the person named, and the house style is surname-or-handle only — the roster is a ship's document, not a directory of identifiable people. Berth, deck, and voyage seed are derived from the name itself rather than stored, so the register is reproducible from the name list alone and nobody's berth moves because a row above them was edited. Entries are reviewed before they are added, because this text renders on a public page and is drawn as a crew name in a game other people play.

THE LONG HAUL is a work of parody. Astraeus Launch Systems, the transit vessel MANIFEST, and the Founder are fictional, and any resemblance to a real launch provider is the joke.
