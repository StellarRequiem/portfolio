Skyburst Privacy Policy

Effective October 29, 2026 · Applies to Skyburst 1.0 for iPhone, iPad and Mac

The short version
  • Skyburst collects no personal data. None.
  • No tracking, no analytics, no advertising, no accounts.
  • The music files you import stay on your device. They are never uploaded.

Data we collect

Skyburst does not collect, store on our servers, transmit, or share any personal information. The app does not connect to the internet for any purpose and contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking software.

Music you import

When you choose a music file for a beat-synced show, Skyburst reads it through the standard file picker on your device. The file is analyzed (beat and tempo detection) and played back entirely on your device. It is never uploaded, copied to any server, or shared with anyone. Skyburst only accesses files you explicitly choose.

Information stored on your device

Skyburst saves a few preferences locally on your device, such as volume, mute, and display options (for example reduced flashing). It may also keep firework recipes and shows that you add to the app's folder. This information stays on your device and is removed when you delete the app.

Device features

On iPhone and iPad, Skyburst may read the device's motion sensors to tilt the camera view. This data is used live inside the app and is not recorded or sent anywhere. Skyburst does not use your camera, microphone, photos, contacts, or location.

Children

Because Skyburst collects no data from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
