Help for Skyburst on iPhone, iPad and Mac

Need help? Email security@xclusivexo.com with your device, OS version and what happened. We aim to reply within a few days.

Getting started

  • Tap or click the sky to launch fireworks, or press Fire.
  • Use the effect picker to choose a firework type, including the signature Grok Bot firework.
  • Open the show menu to play a prepared show.

Music shows

  • Tap Music and choose an audio file from Files (iPhone and iPad) or from the open dialog (Mac).
  • Skyburst analyzes the beat on your device, then builds a show that follows it. Your music never leaves your device.
  • If a track does not work, try a standard format such as MP3, M4A, AAC, WAV or AIFF. Files protected by DRM, such as some streaming downloads, cannot be read.

Sound and flashing

  • No sound? Check the mute button in the app and your device's volume and silent switch.
  • Sensitive to bright flashes? Turn on the reduced-flashing option in the app.

Performance

Skyburst offers quality tiers. If the show stutters on an older device, choose a lower tier.

Requirements

iPhone and iPad: iOS / iPadOS 17.0 or later. Mac: macOS 14.0 or later.

Privacy

Skyburst collects no data. See the Privacy Policy.
